Mastodon timelines, accounts, and threads, as pages. Also GoToSocial, Pleroma, snac, and other servers with the same API.

 mastodon://instance/: local timeline
 mastodon://instance/public: federated timeline
 mastodon://instance/home: home timeline (logged in)
 mastodon://instance/tags/name: hashtag
 mastodon://instance/@user: account's posts
 mastodon://instance/@user/id: post and its thread
 mastodon://instance/login: log in or out

HTTPS through the HTTP proxy, see settings. To post, favorite, or boost, log in with an access token (instance settings: Development, New Application).
