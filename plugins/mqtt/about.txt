MQTT brokers, as pages:

 mqtt://broker/topic: the topic's retained value, and a form to publish to it
 mqtt://broker/a/+/c: each matching topic and its value, as a table
 mqtt://broker/a/b/: every topic under a/b (a/b/#)
 mqtt://broker/: every topic

Write # as %23 in an address. Tables collect messages for the Wait setting (2 seconds at first).

Publishing: QoS 0 or 1, retained or not. Brokers that require a login get Mill's login dialog.

MQTT 3.1.1, port 1883 unless the address names another. No TLS.
